Lil Things

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

This page covers the Lil Things app for iPhone and iPad, and the lilthings.app website. It is written in plain language on purpose.

The short version

  • You sign in with Apple.
  • Drawings, photos, and profile pictures are stored so you can share them with the people in your Spaces.
  • Lil Things can send push notifications.
  • There are no ads.
  • We don’t sell your data.

Sign in with Apple

Lil Things uses Sign in with Apple. Apple shares the account details you agree to share, such as your name and an email address. That email may be a private relay address. We use it to keep you signed in and to tell your Spaces apart.

What the app stores

So a Space can work, the app stores:

  • Drawings you make
  • Photos you send
  • Profile pictures, display names, and accent colors you add
  • The Spaces you share, and the reactions on them

This is so your partner can see what you sent, including on the shared home-screen widget, and so you can replay a drawing the way it was made.

Who can see it

The people you share a Space with can see the drawings, photos, and reactions in that Space. A profile picture and display name help them recognize you. We don’t publish your Spaces anywhere else.

Push notifications

Lil Things can send push notifications, for example when someone in a Space leaves you something or reacts. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings.

Ads and selling data

There are no ads in Lil Things. We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use your drawings, photos, or profile pictures for advertising.

This website

lilthings.app is a static site. It doesn’t ask you to make an account, and it doesn’t use advertising or analytics cookies.

Questions, or a deletion request

Email support@lilthings.app if you have a question, or if you want your information deleted. There’s also a support page.
