Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · Last updated October 18, 2026

Lil Things is a free app made by Eric Leino, an individual developer in California, USA. This policy explains what the Lil Things iPhone and iPad app and the website at lilthings.app collect, why, and the choices you have. It is written so a person can read it. It is also meant to cover the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, and the core rights in the GDPR and UK GDPR.

Contact: support@lilthings.app.

The short version

  • You sign in with Apple only. We never see your Apple ID password.
  • We store what you make and share — drawings and stroke data, photos, notes, reactions, profile details, Spaces, and follows — so the app and the Home Screen widget can work.
  • We use Apple push notifications. We do not use advertising or tracking.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • You can delete your account in the app: Settings, then Account, then Delete my account. You can also email us.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us, information the app creates so a Space or a follow can work, and a small amount of technical data. We do not collect precise location, contacts, health data, financial information, or government ID numbers.

Account, from Sign in with Apple

  • User ID. A stable identifier from Apple for your account.
  • Email. The email you choose to share. If you use Hide My Email, this is a private relay address, not necessarily your real inbox.
  • Display name. The name you share from Apple or set in the app.
  • Handle. The name other people in Lil Things can use to find or recognize you, if you set one.

Profile and content

  • Profile photo, if you add one.
  • Drawings and stroke data. The lines you draw, including the points needed to replay a drawing in the app and to step through frames on the widget.
  • Photos and notes you attach to a little thing.
  • Reactions you leave, such as a heart, a laugh, or a sparkle, and who they were for.
  • Space membership. The Spaces you create or join, their names, and who is in them.
  • Follower relationships. Who you follow, and who follows you, so a shared doodle can reach the right widgets.

Safety and the device

  • Reports and blocks. Reports you submit, content involved in a report, and the blocks you set or that are set against you.
  • Push token. A device token from Apple Push Notification service, so we can deliver notifications you allow. It is not used for ads.
  • Server logs. Basic logs such as IP address, date and time, and request details, used to run and protect the service.

The website does not ask you to create an account. If you email support@lilthings.app, we receive whatever you put in that message, including your email address.

How we use it

We use this information to run Lil Things, and only for that. That means:

  • Creating and securing your account.
  • Storing little things and showing them to the people you meant: members of a Space, or followers, including on Home Screen widgets.
  • Replaying drawings and keeping reactions with the thing they belong to.
  • Sending push notifications you have allowed, such as when someone sends you a little thing, reacts, or draws back.
  • Letting you report and block, and reviewing reports.
  • Keeping the service reliable and safe, including diagnosing errors and abuse.
  • Responding when you write to support, and honoring deletion and privacy requests.

We do not use your drawings, photos, notes, or profile to train machine-learning models. We do not use them for advertising. We do not sell them.

Legal bases

If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Contract. Providing the account, Spaces, follows, widgets, replay, and the other features you ask the app to perform.
  • Legitimate interests. Securing the service, preventing abuse, keeping short server logs, and moderating reports. These interests are limited to running a small private app, and they are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent. Push notifications, which iOS asks you to allow and which you can turn off in iOS Settings. You can also withdraw by deleting the app or the account.
  • Legal obligation. If we must keep or disclose information to comply with law, such as a valid demand related to child-safety reporting.

Processors

We share information with service providers who process it on our instructions to run Lil Things. We do not give them permission to use it for their own advertising.

  • Supabase hosts the app’s database, stored files, and related backend. Account data and content are stored there.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, the App Store, and Apple Push Notification service (APNs). Apple processes your Apple ID under its own terms. We send APNs a device token and the notification payload needed to deliver a notification.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. It does not receive your Lil Things account. Like most hosts, it may keep standard server logs of visits to lilthings.app.

People you share with can see what you send them. Members of a Space can see the little things and reactions in that Space. Followers can see a doodle you share with followers. A display name, handle, and profile photo help them recognize you. We don’t publish your Spaces on a public website.

We may also disclose information if we believe in good faith that the law requires it, or when it is necessary to investigate a report of illegal content, including content that sexually exploits a child.

No sale or ad sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not allow third parties to collect information across other apps or websites for advertising. There are no ads in Lil Things and no tracking SDKs.

Because we do not sell or share personal information in the CPRA sense, there is no sale or share to opt out of. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first and provide the opt-out the law requires.

Retention

  • Account and content are kept while your account is open, so your Spaces, follows, and widgets keep working.
  • Push tokens are kept until they are replaced, you turn notifications off, or you delete the account.
  • Server logs and IP addresses are kept for up to 90 days, then deleted or aggregated, unless we need a specific log longer to investigate abuse or a security incident.
  • Reports and blocks are kept while they are needed to enforce the community guidelines and the terms, and then deleted. A record of a serious safety issue, including child exploitation, may be kept longer where the law requires it.
  • Support emails are kept as long as needed to handle the request and keep a record of it, then deleted.

Deletion

You can delete your account in the app:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Choose Delete my account.

You can also email support@lilthings.app from the address on the account, or tell us the Apple relay address, and ask us to delete it. We may ask for enough information to confirm the request is yours.

After a deletion request, we delete or de-identify personal information from active systems within 30 days, and from backups within 90 days. Exceptions, used only as far as needed:

  • Information we must keep for a legal obligation, security, or to document enforcement of the community guidelines.
  • Little things already delivered to someone else may remain with that person until they delete them. Deleting your account does not reach into their camera roll or screenshots.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what we hold, to correct it, to delete it, to receive a copy, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Email support@lilthings.app with the request. We respond within 30 days for GDPR and UK GDPR requests, and within 45 days for CCPA requests. If a law allows a longer period and we need it, we will tell you why.

You may use an authorized agent where the law allows. We may ask the agent for proof of authority and, if the law permits, confirm the request with you.

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. We would like the chance to help first.

California

This section is for California residents and uses the categories in the CCPA / CPRA. In the last 12 months we collect, and have collected, these categories for the purposes in this policy:

  • Identifiers, such as name, handle, user ID, email address, push token, and IP address.
  • Customer records, such as a profile photo.
  • Internet or network activity, limited to basic server logs.
  • User content and communications, such as drawings, stroke data, photos, notes, and reactions.
  • Inferences: we do not draw inferences about you for advertising or profiling.

We do not collect precise geolocation, commercial purchase history (the app is free and has no in-app purchases), or protected-classification information as a matter of course. Content you choose to draw or upload might include sensitive details about you. Account email and the contents of what you send can be sensitive personal information under the CPRA. We use that information only to provide Lil Things, which is a purpose that does not require a separate “limit the use of my sensitive personal information” opt-out. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

We do not sell or share personal information, including the personal information of anyone under 16. We have no actual knowledge of selling the personal information of minors. We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing, so the Shine the Light request is not applicable.

California rights include the right to know, delete, and correct, and the right to opt out of sale or sharing. Because we do not sell or share, that opt-out does not change what the app does. To use the rights that do apply, email support@lilthings.app or delete the account in Settings.

EEA, UK, and Switzerland

Eric Leino is the controller of personal data described in this policy. There is no separate EU or UK representative. You can exercise your rights by emailing support@lilthings.app.

Your rights include access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing. Lil Things does not make decisions about you by automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Children

Lil Things is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. You must be at least 13 to use the app. If you are under the age at which you can consent to data processing where you live, you may use Lil Things only with permission from a parent or guardian, as described in the terms.

If we learn that we have personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it. Parents and guardians can email support@lilthings.app and ask us to delete it. Please include enough detail for us to find the account.

Security

We protect information in transit with HTTPS. Access to the Supabase project is limited. Sign in with Apple means we do not store an Apple ID password. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Please use a device passcode, and be thoughtful about what you put in a drawing or a photo.

International transfers

Eric Leino is in the United States. Supabase and Apple may process information in the United States and in other countries where they operate. Those countries may not provide the same data protection rules as your home country.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR requires a transfer tool, we use an appropriate safeguard, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or the UK international data transfer addendum, or we rely on an adequacy decision when one applies to the recipient. You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguard by emailing support@lilthings.app.

Cookies and Do Not Track

The website is static. We do not set our own cookies, and we do not use analytics or advertising cookies. We do not track you across other sites or apps. A Do Not Track or global privacy control signal does not change the site’s behavior, because there is no tracking to turn off. The iOS app does not use IDFA and does not ask to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date. If a change is material, we will also give notice in the app or by email when we have an email for the account. The version that applies is the one posted on the date you use Lil Things.
