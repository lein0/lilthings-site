Support

Lil Things is free, for iPhone and iPad on iOS 17 or later. You sign in with Apple. If something feels stuck, write to support@lilthings.app and include what you expected, what happened, and your device and iOS version.

Common questions

How do widgets work?

A Lil Things widget lives on the Home Screen, beside your other apps. Small, medium, and large are all Home Screen sizes. The widget shows a little thing from a Space you have pinned, or a doodle shared with you as a follower. Drawings can step through their replay frames on the widget. Open the app for the full Watch It Happen playback, stroke by stroke. Photos stay photos. A heart, a laugh, or a sparkle can be left on the widget.

How do I add a widget?
  1. Touch and hold the Home Screen until the icons jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Lil Things.
  4. Choose small, medium, or large, and choose the Space.
  5. Place it, then tap Done.

How do I pin a Space?

Pinning chooses which Space a widget keeps showing. When you add a widget, pick the Space. To change it later, open that Space in the app and pin it to the widget. The widget stays on that Space until you pin a different one. A private Space is still invite-only. Just us is for two people. The crew is for up to ten.

What is Draw back?

Draw back is a reply you draw. Open what someone sent and tap Draw back, make the reply, and send it. It can play on their widget the same way theirs played on yours.

How do notifications work?

If you allow notifications, Lil Things can send a push when someone sends you a little thing, reacts, or draws back. The note can appear on the lock screen. That is a notification, not a lock-screen widget. Turn notifications off anytime in iOS Settings, under Notifications, then Lil Things.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the little thing or the person’s profile, then choose Report or Block. Blocking stops that person from sending you new little things. We review reports within 24 hours and may remove the content, suspend the account, or ban it. You can also email support@lilthings.app. The rules are in the community guidelines.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings, then Account, then Delete my account. You can also email support@lilthings.app from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. What we delete, and how long backups can take, is in the privacy policy.
