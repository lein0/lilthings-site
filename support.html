Support

We’re glad you’re here. If something feels stuck, write to support@lilthings.app and we’ll help.

Lil Things is for iPhone and iPad, on iOS 17 or later.

Get it on the App Store (opens in a new tab)

Add the widget

  1. Touch and hold the Home Screen.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Lil Things.
  4. Pick a size — small, medium, or large — and choose the Space.

A Space with someone

A Space is for two people. Invite your person, give the Space a name if you like, and send a drawing or a photo. It shows up for them, including on the widget.

Drawings, photos, and replay

Drawings can be replayed with Watch It Happen, stroke by stroke, in the order you made them. Photos stay as photos.

Reactions

A heart, a hug, a sparkle, or a smile. You can leave one on the widget, or on the thing they sent.

Your profile

Add a photo, a display name, and an accent color so they know it’s you. Quiet hours keep notifications for later. You sign in with Apple.

If you write to us

It helps to include:

  • What you expected to happen
  • What happened instead
  • Your device, and the version of iOS

Questions about what we store are on the privacy policy.
