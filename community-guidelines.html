Lil Things

Community Guidelines

Effective October 18, 2026

Lil Things is a small place for small things: a drawing on someone’s Home Screen, a note, a photo for your people. These guidelines keep it kind. They are part of the Terms of Use.

The short version. Be kind. Don’t hurt people, don’t scare them, and don’t send anything sexual, hateful, or illegal. If something is wrong, report it and block the person. We look at reports within 24 hours.

Be kind

Send someone a little thing because you like them, not because you want a reaction at their expense. A Space is private. Followers still didn’t ask for cruelty. Assume there is a person on the other side of the widget.

Harassment and bullying

No insults meant to shame someone, no piling on, no repeated unwanted contact after they have asked you to stop, and no threats. Drawing someone just to mock them counts. If they block you, leave them alone.

Hate

No hate. Don’t attack people, or encourage others to attack them, because of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, disability, disease, age, sex, sexual orientation, or gender identity. Slurs and symbols used that way are not allowed, even inside a joke or a doodle.

Sexual content involving minors

Zero tolerance. Sexual content involving anyone under 18 is forbidden. That includes drawings, photos, jokes, and requests. We remove it, we permanently ban the account, and we report apparent child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to law enforcement when the law requires it.

Do not send us copies of that content by email. Report it in the app, or email support@lilthings.app with where to find it and what you saw, without attaching the material.

Nudity and sexual content

Lil Things is not a place for nudity, pornography, or sexual solicitation. Keep drawings and photos clothed and non-sexual. This applies in a private Space too.

Violence and threats

No threats of violence, no instructions for harming someone, and no graphic violence. Don’t use a little thing to intimidate a person, a Space, or a follower.

Self-harm

Don’t post content that encourages suicide, self-harm, or eating disorders, and don’t mock someone who is struggling. We remove that content.

If you are in crisis in the United States, call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. If you are elsewhere, contact a local emergency number or a local crisis line.

Illegal activity

Don’t use Lil Things to plan, sell, or promote anything illegal, including drugs, weapons, fraud, or stolen material. Don’t ask anyone for money under false pretenses.

Spam

Don’t flood a Space or your followers with the same thing, don’t send people unsolicited promotions, and don’t use the app as a bulk mailing list. Followers are not an advertising channel.

Impersonation

Don’t pretend to be someone else. A display name, handle, or profile photo should not be used to trick people into thinking you are a friend, a public figure, or Eric Leino.

Intellectual property

Don’t send drawings, photos, or notes that use someone else’s work unless you have the right to share it. If someone is using your work without permission, see how to write to us in the terms.

Privacy

Don’t share another person’s private information. That includes a home address, a phone number, a private email, an identification document, a private photo they didn’t agree to share, or a screenshot of a private conversation from somewhere else. A Space being small does not make it fine.

How to report

In the app, open the little thing or the person’s profile and choose Report. Say what happened. You can also email support@lilthings.app with:

  • Who was involved (a handle or display name helps).
  • What you saw, and roughly when.
  • A screenshot, if you can take one safely. Never attach sexual content of a minor.

We review reports within 24 hours.

How to block

In the app, open the person’s profile and choose Block. Blocking stops that person from sending you new little things. You can report them at the same time. If a blocked person tries to reach you through a new account, report that account too.

What happens next

Depending on what we find, we may:

  • Remove the little thing.
  • Warn the person.
  • Suspend the account for a while.
  • Ban the account.

Serious harm, sexual content involving a minor, and credible threats can be a ban on the first report. We may also keep a record of the report as described in the Privacy Policy.

Appeals

If we removed something you sent, or suspended or banned you, and you think it was a mistake, email support@lilthings.app with “Appeal” in the subject. Tell us what was removed and why you believe it follows these guidelines. We will read it. We don’t promise to restore an account.

These can change

If the guidelines change, the new version will be posted here with a new date. The terms explain how changes work.
