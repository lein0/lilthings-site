The service

Lil Things is an iPhone app. It lets you send little drawings, photos, and notes into a Space. Spaces are invite-only and can include more than two people. You can also turn on a public profile and broadcast drawings to your followers. Those broadcasts are visible to your followers, including on their Home Screen widgets. What you send inside a Space stays in that Space.

Widgets are Home Screen widgets in small, medium, and large sizes. A drawing can replay in the app, stroke by stroke, and the widget can step through those frames. You can leave a reaction, draw back, and receive a push notification if you allow notifications. Features can change as the app is updated.
