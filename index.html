How it works

Three small steps.

That’s the whole idea. A Space, a little thing, and a widget.

  1. 01

    Open a Space

    Invite one person. A Space is just the two of you — your corner, with nobody else in it.

  2. 02

    Leave something little

    A drawing, a photo, a note in your handwriting. It does not have to be good. It has to be from you.

  3. 03

    Let them find it

    It shows up on their widget. They can react right there, or watch the drawing appear, stroke by stroke.

FAQ

Questions, answered gently.

What is Lil Things?

A shared home-screen widget for two people. You send little drawings and photos into a Space, and they show up on each other’s phone.

Who is a Space for?

Two people. A partner, a friend, someone you like to send small things to. You can keep more than one Space.

Which phones and tablets?

iPhone and iPad, on iOS 17 or later. Both of you need the app.

What is Watch It Happen?

It’s a replay of a drawing, stroke by stroke, in the order it was made. Photos stay as photos.

How do reactions work?

You can leave a heart, a hug, a sparkle, or a smile on the widget, or on the thing they sent. It’s a small way to say you saw it.

What happens to my drawings and photos?

They’re stored so you can share them with the people in your Spaces, including on the widget. Sign in is with Apple. There are no ads, and we don’t sell your data. The privacy policy has the rest.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Lil Things.

How do I get help?

Write to support@lilthings.app, or visit the support page.
